Highlights

The good stuff, in motion.

Image quality

Crisp, whichever way you render.

Every project picks the pipeline that suits it. Render forward with MSAA for razor-clean edges at a low cost, or deferred with temporal anti-aliasing for dense lighting and fine detail, with SMAA and CMAA2 in between. Upscale with DLSS, FSR or the engine's own upscaler when you need headroom, and frames are paced to the display so play feels responsive on any screen.

  • Forward or deferred shading
  • MSAA, SMAA, CMAA2 or TAA
  • NVIDIA DLSS, AMD FSR or our own upscaler, with dynamic resolution
  • Low-latency frame pacing with Reflex and Anti-Lag
  • Graphics settings picked for each player's PC
  • Shaders ready before you play

Destruction

Everything breaks beautifully.

Car bodies dent where they're hit and stay dented. Soft things squish and wobble, props shatter, scaffolding comes down plank by plank and rope fences catch you and throw you back. All of it is set up in the editor, not written by hand.

  • Crash damage and squish
  • Smashable props and collapsing structures
  • Rope fences and swinging obstacles
  • Debris that tumbles, rolls and settles

Editor

Hit Play. You're in the game.

Play runs your real game right inside the editor, menus, HUD and all. Stop, tweak a value or a mesh, and you're back in. Code changes land in the running editor almost instantly.

  • Play in the viewport or its own window
  • Hot reload for your game code
  • Undo for every edit

Tuning

Tune the feel while you play.

Tag any value in your code and it shows up in the Tune pane. Drag it while the game runs and feel the difference on the next corner. The pane shows what you changed since your last commit and saves versions you can go back to.

  • One attribute to expose a value
  • Live edits during Play
  • Changes marked, versions saved

Multiplayer

Online multiplayer, built right in.

Mark a value to sync it and a method to call it across the network, and you're online. Go server-authoritative so results are consistent and cheat-resistant, or client-authoritative so each player simulates their own objects. Either way, every player's own actions feel instant. Test a whole match with several players inside the editor.

  • Server- or client-authoritative
  • Prediction and rollback
  • Lag compensation
  • Parties, rejoin and seamless host handover
  • Steam lobbies, invites and relay

Replays

Every knockout gets its replay.

Instant replays and killcams are part of the engine. Show the last few seconds from any camera, slow it down, cut back to the action. They're light enough to leave on all the time.

  • Instant replays and killcams
  • Any camera, any moment
  • Works in online matches

Time travel

Rewind any play session. Then take over.

Every time you press Play, the session is recorded. Scrub back to the moment something went wrong, step through it frame by frame, and grab the controls from right there. Bugs that happen once can be watched again and again.

  • Seek, step and take over
  • Every session recorded automatically
  • Shows exactly where a run went differently

Modelling

A modelling suite, right in the level.

Block out and finish geometry where it lives, with the tools you reach for every day. Stack modifiers to subdivide, mirror or cut without losing a single edit. Collision follows the mesh as you work: cut a hole and players can fall through it.

  • Extrude, bevel, inset, bridge, loops and cuts
  • Modifier stack: subdivision, mirror, booleans, array, bevel
  • UV editor and automatic unwrapping
  • Soft selection, vertex colours, curves and pipes

Texturing Preview

Paint with materials, layer by layer.

Texture a model the way a painter works: stack smart materials, fills and masks, and watch rust gather in the creases and paint wear off the edges. Drop stickers straight onto the model in the viewport. Every layer stays editable, and the stack updates live.

  • Smart materials and smart masks
  • Stickers placed in the viewport
  • Live, GPU-composited layers
  • Built from your own texture graphs

Texture graphs Preview

Procedural materials, node by node.

A full node-based material editor inside the engine: noises, patterns, filters, blends and PBR outputs, with a live 3D preview. Bake a high-poly model down, texture a mesh from its own shape, or describe what you want and an AI agent edits the graph for you.

  • Hundreds of nodes
  • High-poly bakes, rounded edges and panels from the mesh
  • Hotspot sheets for trims and panels
  • Imports Substance Designer graphs

Hotspotting

Trim sheets that apply themselves.

Model a shape and its faces find the right part of a hotspot sheet on their own: trims, panels and bevels land where they belong, at a consistent texel density. Level art that looks finished while you're still blocking it out.

  • Hotspot, strip and pipe mapping
  • Consistent texel density
  • Hotspot sheets made in texture graphs

Foliage Preview

Grow your own trees.

Trees, bushes and grass grow from generators you shape with a few sliders: trunk, branches, leaves and the gaps between them. Leaves are built from real vein structure, light glows through them, and the whole plant sways and flutters in the scene's wind.

  • Tree, conifer, bush and grass generators
  • Procedural leaves and textured bark
  • Wind, translucency and canopy shading

AI modelling Experimental

Ask for a bar stool. Get a bar stool.

Describe a model and an AI agent builds it with the editor's own modelling tools: it blocks it out, checks its renders and refines. The result is ordinary, editable geometry, and it can change models you made too.

  • Uses the same tools you do
  • Plain, editable meshes
  • Edits the selected model on request

Realtime GI Preview

Light that bounces in real time.

Move the sun, a lamp or a wall and the bounce light follows at once, with no bake. Ray-traced probes fill the world and a per-pixel final gather keeps corners and contact shading crisp.

  • Hardware ray-traced probes
  • Per-pixel final gather
  • Dynamic sun, lights and geometry

Live lighting

Baked lighting with no Bake button.

Prefer baked? Move a wall and the lightmaps rebake in the background while you keep working. The GPU baker uses hardware ray tracing and denoising, with reflection probes that fit their rooms.

  • Background rebakes while you edit
  • Hardware ray-traced bakes
  • Directional lightmaps and light probes

Audio

Sound design without the round trips.

Layer sounds on a timeline, add a little random pitch and volume, and hear it straight away. Mix the whole game with buses, snapshots and ducking, and give engines a voice that rises and falls with every rev.

  • Sound Designer with live preview
  • Mixer, snapshots, ducking and reverb zones
  • Engine sounds that follow revs and throttle
  • Doppler and occlusion

Game UI

Game UI you can scrub like a timeline.

Author HUDs and menus as widgets, then preview every state and animation without starting the game. The code that connects them to your game is generated for you.

  • Visual widget authoring
  • Live preview with sample data
  • Localization built in
